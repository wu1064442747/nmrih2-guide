## Page Acceptance Checklist

- A reader can complete a decision or task after reading the page.
- Facts name their source, game version, and last verified date.
- Map and database pages use a table, image, diagram, or verified capture.
- The page links to at least three relevant guides where those guides exist.
- Version-sensitive advice is reviewed after every official update.
